## About me

I'm halfway through a Bachelor's in **Software Engineering** at the Federal University of Pampa (UNIPAMPA), Campus Alegrete. I have hands-on experience in systems development, process automation and requirements engineering, in academic projects, outreach programs and with a real client.

I'm especially interested in where **artificial intelligence meets psychology**: technology that helps people build better habits and use technology itself more consciously.

- 🔭 **PET-Saúde e-Cidadania** (Ministry of Health / MEC): bringing software engineering to digital health in the public health system (SUS). Field visits, requirements (SRS) and AI-assisted mobile prototyping of the *App SAE*
- 🛠️ **Academic Management Support** (UNIPAMPA/PROGRAD): Python + Playwright automation that collects course syllabi from the university system and organizes them in Google Drive
- 🤖 Exploring AI-assisted engineering and autonomous agents (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Agno)
- 🌎 Portuguese (native) · English (intermediate) · Spanish (basic)
